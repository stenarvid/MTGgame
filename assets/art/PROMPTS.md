# Anime card art

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool. Original PNG outputs are bundled here.
The five original character illustrations depict their named cards and also serve
as commander and token portraits. All 25 named nonland cards now have individual
illustrations mapped in `cards.json`; the 20 additional prompts are recorded in
`INDIVIDUAL_PROMPTS.md`. The environment is used for the menu, battlefield backdrop,
and land cards.
These are original fantasy characters, not licensed franchise characters.
